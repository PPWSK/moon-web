How It Works

The update has two parts:

  1. Turn on your MOON and wait for it to boot (~5s)
  2. Tap Connect and select your MOON device
  3. Tap Start Update — part 1 runs automatically
  4. When part 1 finishes, tap Connect again to reconnect
  5. Tap Start Update Part 2 to complete the update
For MOON Ninety this is a single transfer — there is no part 2. It takes a few minutes; leave this page open.
Keep your phone/computer close to the MOON and don't switch apps during transfer.
